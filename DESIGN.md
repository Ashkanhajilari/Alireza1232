## Brand & Style

This design system expresses timeless imperial elegance through a disciplined, contemporary lens. Crafted for an ultra-luxury Persian jewelry house, the aesthetic balances modern architectural minimalism with classical Middle Eastern craftsmanship.

The visual language evokes poise, heritage, and exclusivity. Whitespace is generous and deliberate, honoring each gold piece as a museum artifact. Contrast is sharp yet refined: luminous pearl surfaces hold warm metallic reflections, framed by obsidian accents and hairline gold filigree. The UI communicates calm authority, impeccable craftsmanship, and trust, tailored for discerning collectors, heirloom patrons, and fine jewelry buyers.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from polished yellow gold set against raw black velvet and hand-cast architectural plaster.

- **Primary (`#C5A059`, accents `#D4AF37`, `#E5C378`):** The house gold. Used strictly for focal elements, status indicators, active states, borders, and tactile accents.
- **Secondary (`#0B0B0B`, surface variant `#141414`):** Obsidian black. Provides anchor contrast for premium buttons, navigation bars, footer baselines, and editorial hero moments.
- **Tertiary (`#9A7B38`):** Deep burnished gold. Deployed for subtle icon strokes, micro-borders on active surfaces, and small-caps captions requiring high legibility against pale backgrounds.
- **Neutral Surface (`#FAF8F5`, muted `#F5F2EB`):** Iridescent pearl and ivory. Serves as the canvas to let gold photography glow naturally without clinical starkness.
- **Text & Stroke Tiers:** Primary reading text sits at rich charcoal (`#1F1F1F`), secondary details at muted slate (`#4A4A4A`), and delicate divider rules at hairline parchment (`#E8E3DA` and `#DFD7CA`).

## Typography

The typography strategy harmonizes Persian calligraphy principles with Latin luxury editorial design:

- **RTL & Script Hierarchy:** In localized Persian deployment, headings utilize high-contrast Nastaliq/Naskh-inspired serif styles (exemplified by classic Persian display cuts like Vazirmatn and Shabnam in weighted headline treatments), complemented by clean geometric sans-serif for numerical rates, weight specs, and transactional labels.
- **Numerals:** Financial figures, gold carats, and gram weights use tabular layout formatting to maintain absolute vertical alignment across ticker cards and cart ledgers.
- **Case & Proportion:** Sub-labels, carat badges, and metadata utilize letter-spacing expansion in Latin characters, while Persian metadata emphasizes balanced baseline padding and uncompressed diacritics.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is governed by an architectural 12-column grid system built around symmetry, airy margins, and RTL-first directionality.

- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns with 24px gutters and generous 48px canvas padding. Content max-width is anchored at 1360px to preserve intimate viewing proportions.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8 columns with 20px gutters and 32px canvas margins. Catalogs transition to 2-column showcasing.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** 4 columns with 16px gutters and 20px edge margins. Horizontal rail gestures allow jewelry showcases to extend edge-to-edge with peek margins.
- **RTL Geometry:** Visual weight starts at the top-right. Reading vectors, price tags, purity seals, and action arrows default to right-to-left orientation without compromising mathematical grid lines.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy shadows, relying on tonal hierarchy, delicate edge definitions, and diffused warmth:

- **Surface Levels:**
  - *Base Surface:* Pearl Ivory (`#FAF8F5`).
  - *Elevated Card Layer:* Crisp warm white (`#FFFFFF`) framed by hairline border (`1px solid #E8E3DA`).
  - *Overlay Layer:* Midnight Velvet (`#0B0B0B` with 95% opacity) for curated showcases and navigation drawers.
- **Ambient Illumination:** Where elevation is necessary (such as sticky purchase bars or modal dialogs), shadows mimic warm boutique gallery lighting: `0 12px 36px -8px rgba(11, 11, 11, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.08)`.
- **Metallic Borders:** Select flagship cards and badges use a 1px boundary tinted with gold leaf (`#C5A059` at 40% opacity) instead of elevation to signify artisan tiering.

## Shapes

The geometric identity is defined by subtle, razor-precise edges that echo diamond-cut gold bars and bespoke velvet presentation boxes.

- **Soft Form (`0.25rem` / `4px` base):** Inputs, image containers, buttons, and display cards feature subtle edge softening to maintain modern rigor without brutalist severity.
- **Purity Badges & Pills:** Limited strictly to gold purity tags (e.g., عیار ۱۸) and ticker micro-chips, which use full capsule contours to contrast with the architectural grid.
- **Visual Ratios:** Product imagery strictly adheres to a 4:5 vertical proportion, echoing classical portraiture and haute horlogerie framing.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Obsidian Gold):** Solid black (`#0B0B0B`) fill, warm white text, and an inner hairline gold highlight. Hover state shifts to a subtle gold outline (`#C5A059`) with an elegant 0.995 scale compression.
- **Secondary (Bespoke Outline):** Transparent fill, 1px border in `#C5A059`, dark charcoal typography. Hover fills with `#FAF8F5` and deepens the stroke to `#9A7B38`.
- **Tertiary (Text Action):** Understated text accompanied by a fine trailing arrow (`←` in RTL) with an animated underline starting from the right.

### Jewelry Cards
- **Structure:** Clean 4:5 aspect ratio background in `#F5F2EB` with seamless isolation of the gold piece.
- **Metadata Stack:** Top right displays the gold purity badge; top left contains the subtle wishlist icon (hairline stroke).
- **Typography:** Product title in Persian serif, followed by gold weight in grams (گرم) in tabular digits, and final price in Persian numerals with clear currency notation (تومان).

### Gold Purity Badge (نشان عیار ۱۸)
- Compact capsule or squared pill featuring a soft metallic border (`#C5A059`), champagne tint background (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.1)`), and bold, ultra-refined Arabic/Persian numerals (`عیار ۱۸`).

### Live Gold Price Ticker (نرخ لحظه‌ای طلا)
- A sleek horizontal ribbon at the header or top bar.
- Charcoal background (`#141414`) with warm gold and ivory monospaced numerals.
- Displays dynamic indicators: 18K Gold Gram (گرم ۱۸ عیار), Coin (سکه تمام / بهار آزادی), and 24K benchmark, complete with micro-trend carets (green/amber) and live millisecond update pulses.

### Inputs & Selectors
- Pale ivory background (`#FFFFFF`) framed by `#DFD7CA`. Focus state produces a crisp `#C5A059` 1px border with no aggressive browser glow. Floating labels align to the right in Persian text.

### Selection Controls
- Checkboxes and radio buttons feature gold-filled active states (`#C5A059`) with stark white checkmark icons, set against ivory canvases.
